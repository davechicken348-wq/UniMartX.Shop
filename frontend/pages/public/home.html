Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about starting your student business on UnimartX.

Do I need to be a registered business to sell?

Not at all. Any student can create a store in minutes — no registration, no paperwork, no website required. Just your products and your hustle.

How much does it cost to start?

Getting started is free. You can open your store, list products, and share it with your campus at no cost. Paid plans unlock extras as you grow.

How do I get paid?

Customers can pay online or send their order through WhatsApp and Instagram. Track every sale and payout right from your UnimartX dashboard.

Can I sell beyond my campus?

Yes. Your store works online and in person, locally and globally — so you can reach buyers across campus and far beyond.
